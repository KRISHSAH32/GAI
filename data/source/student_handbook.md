# University Academic Regulations & Student Handbook (August 2026)

## 1. Credit & Progression Requirements
- **Graduation Requirement**: Minimum 160 credits required for BTech / BSc degree completion.
- **Semester Credit Load**: Minimum 16 credits, maximum 24 credits per semester. Overloading beyond 24 credits requires Dean approval and CGPA >= 3.5.
- **Grading System**: A+ (10), A (9), B+ (8), B (7), C+ (6), C (5), D (4), F (0 - Fail).
- **Academic Probation Policy**:
  - A student whose Semester GPA falls below 2.0 or Cumulative GPA (CGPA) falls below 2.2 is placed on **Academic Probation**.
  - Students on Academic Probation are restricted to a maximum of 18 credits per semester and cannot register for advanced electives or overload credits.
  - Failure to lift probation within 2 consecutive semesters results in academic suspension.

## 2. Course Registration, Add/Drop & Retake Policy
- **Course Add/Drop**: Allowed only during the first 2 calendar weeks of the semester with Faculty Advisor approval.
- **Retake Policy for Failed Courses**:
  - Core courses with grade 'F' MUST be retaken in the next semester in which the course is offered.
  - A failed prerequisite course invalidates registration for any advanced course that explicitly lists it as a prerequisite.
  - Maximum 2 retake attempts per course permitted.
- **Attendance Policy**:
  - Minimum **75% attendance** is mandatory in every registered course to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
  - Attendance between 65%-74% requires Medical Certificate approval from Director of Campus Life. Below 65% results in automatic 'F' grade due to shortage.

ABOUT UNIVERSITY 
 
Vidyashilp University is the culmination of an outstanding legacy of the Vidyashilp 
Education Group, committed to excellence in education. Vidyashilp University aims to 
foster an environment and culture of collaboration, responsiveness, high performance and 
service. We are people-centred and have a strongly desire to provide a unique Vidyashilp 
Experience to our students and staff, focused on nurturing students who will become self-
aware, confident, empathetic and resourceful leaders who will serve society to the best of 
their abilities. 
 
The VU Experience fosters a climate for collaboration between individuals and groups from 
diverse cultures and backgrounds. VU creates a diverse, inclusive community and 
environment where VU faculty, staff, students and parents, and alumni feel connected to 
one another by a common sense of purpose, pride, and spirit, and develop a distinctive 
ability to work, learn, lead and serve in diverse contexts. 
 
VU’s primary educational goal is the cultivation of deep domain knowledge within each 
student’s chosen field. We believe that investing in all domains of our differentiated focus 
areas is essential to create intellectual capital to drive progress and achieve excellence. At 
VU, our schools don’t function in silos but rather with an interdisciplinary dependence. 
We focus on a problem -solving approach and provide program choices across all four 
schools with a curriculum established in Data Science, Digital Business, Design Studies, 
Legal Studies and Liberal Arts.

1 ADMISSION
Admission to the Universityshall be open to all persons irrespective of caste, class,
creed, gender, abilities, or nationality. All admissions shall be made on the basis
of merit in the qualifying examinations and as per the rules and guidelines
prescribed by the Government of Karnataka, other concerned regulatory bodies
and the entrance examinations conducted by the University.
The students shall be admitted to a Program subject to fulfilment of eligibility
criteria, as prescribed by the University for the respective Program.
A student admitted to a Program of study, shall continue to remain registered for
such a Program, till they successfully complete the Program or withdraw from the
Program in accordance with the concerned Program Regulations.
Every student duly admitted to the University for a Program after compliance with
prescribed formalities and payment of prescribed fees as applicable and other
amounts and submission of prescribed documents and certificates shall be allotted
a University Enrolment Number OR UEN.
If a student fails to pay the University Fee, or fails to produce all the mandatory
documents and certificates required for admission to the University before the
prescribed last date, the Provisional Offer of Admission to the student shall stand
withdrawn.
If a student desires to join the University on the basis of Lateral Entry or Transfer
of Credits from other Institutes/Universities during the Program, they shall be
examined for eligibility for admission as per the procedure and criteria laid down
in the Academic Regulations of the University and the concerned Program
Regulations, and admission shall be dependent on their eligibility so determined.
Confirmation of admission and enrolment in the University is subject to fulfilment
of the eligibility criteria.

Vidyashilp University is committed for providing the best education opportunities to 
motivated students. The University has established various categories of scholarships to 
encourage and enable students to pursue education at Vidyashilp University.  
Scholarships are provided to reward and support meritorious and talented students, who 
have demonstrated exceptional performance in academics, sports and co-curricular 
activities. The University is particularly committed to enabling meritorious students with 
financial constraints, differently abled students and achievers at the state and national 
levels, through appropriate scholarships.
